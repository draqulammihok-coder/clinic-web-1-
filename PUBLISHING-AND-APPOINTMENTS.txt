AURELIA DENTAL STUDIO — PUBLISHING NOTES

PUBLISH ALL FILES
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Upload the complete 01-dental-clinic folder contents to your hosting document root.
3. Keep the assets folder and all CSS/JS files in their original locations.
4. Do not upload only index.html. The website contains 32 HTML pages.
5. Open index.html (or your hosted domain) and use Treatments to browse all 24 treatment pages.
6. Every page includes the shared site navigation and page directory.

APPOINTMENT FORM
- Every treatment page's appointment button opens contact.html with its treatment preselected.
- The form includes name, email, phone, treatment, appointment type, preferred date, preferred time and optional notes.
- Form requests are saved in localStorage on the visitor's device for this demo.
- This does NOT send email or confirm an appointment. Connect a secure backend / booking service before accepting real patient bookings.
- Dentist names and contact details in the demo directory are sample data.

The treatment information is educational and should be reviewed by a licensed dental professional before publication.

HERO DESIGN
- The homepage hero uses the local optimized assets/aurelia-hero.webp image.
- Decorative proof badges and the scroll/progress strip have been removed.
- Keep the assets folder when publishing.
