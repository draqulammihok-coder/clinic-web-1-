DENTAL TREATMENT · 12 / 24

Endodontics

Specialist care for the dental pulp and tissues around the roots of teeth.

Request an appointment ↗

What is this treatment?

The clinician evaluates tooth pain, sensitivity and signs of pulp or root-area disease.

Who may need it?

  • Deep decay
  • dental trauma
  • lingering sensitivity
  • root infection

How treatment may work

  • Diagnostic testing
  • imaging when needed and a tailored pulp or root treatment plan.

Potential benefits and considerations

  • Aims to preserve natural teeth when the pulp or root tissues are affected.
  • Your dentist will explain expected outcomes, limitations and alternatives.

Related options

  • Root canal treatment
  • vital pulp therapy
  • endodontic retreatment

Before you decide

Ask about expected visits, healing or adjustment time, possible risks, costs, alternatives and long-term maintenance. The right plan depends on an in-person examination.
