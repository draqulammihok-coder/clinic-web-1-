Teeth Whitening

Professional guidance and supervised options to lighten natural tooth shade.

Request an appointment ↗

What is this treatment?

A dental check identifies the cause of discoloration and checks for cavities, gum problems or sensitivity.

Who may need it?

  • Surface stains
  • age-related discoloration
  • selected yellowing

How treatment may work

  • Shade assessment
  • gum protection and in-office or dentist-supervised home whitening.

Potential benefits and considerations

  • May lighten some external and internal stains; results vary by tooth and cause of discoloration.
  • Your dentist will explain expected outcomes, limitations and alternatives.

Related options

  • In-office whitening
  • custom-tray whitening

Before you decide

Ask about expected visits, healing or adjustment time, possible risks, costs, alternatives and long-term maintenance. The right plan depends on an in-person examination.
